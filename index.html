The Ark Inn story

A private house with the spirit of the valley.

Ark Inn was imagined as a softer way to experience Srinagar: intimate in scale, generous in feeling, and shaped by timber, mountain light, and the changing garden outside.

There is no rush here. Mornings begin slowly, afternoons drift into the garden, and every detail is considered without ever feeling formal.
